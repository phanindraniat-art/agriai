Leaf Spot (Colletotrichum capsici / Early Blight)

Severity: Moderate (Level 2)

Symptoms Observed:

Elliptical brown necrotic patches on leaves with chlorotic yellow halo.

Recommended Chemical Treatment:

Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 ml/L in 500 liters water per acre.

Organic & Biological Control:

Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or Trichoderma viride culture @ 10 g/L.
